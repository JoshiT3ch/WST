A SMALL IDEA, WITH ROOM TO GROW

Let’s keep
the good things going.

Finds began as a student project exploring how a simple marketplace could make discovering second-hand clothing feel inviting and easy.

Our vision is a space where people can pass on pieces they no longer wear and discover something that feels like them. A place for personal style, thoughtful choices, and everyday connections.

This website is our first step: a frontend-only preview of that idea, made with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

WHAT WE BELIEVE IN

A few things worth keeping.

Style with personality

The best finds feel like you. We celebrate individual taste and pieces with their own character.

A longer life for clothes

Rewearing and passing things on keeps useful pieces in wardrobes and out of the forgotten pile.

People at the heart

Behind every find is a person. We want the experience to feel clear, welcoming, and easy to understand.
