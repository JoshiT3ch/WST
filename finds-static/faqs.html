The basics

What is Finds?

Finds is a student project inspired by second-hand clothing marketplaces. The idea is to help people discover pre-loved pieces and give good clothes a new home.

Is this a working marketplace?

This is a student prototype with a Node.js backend for sample listings and contact messages. Real sellers, accounts, and transactions are planned for a future version.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse listings and send the Finds team a message without signing up or logging in.

Browsing & saving

Are these items available to buy?

No. All items, prices, conditions, and illustrations are samples for the prototype. No real inventory is offered for sale.

How do I explore the finds?

On the Home page, scroll to the featured collection. Use the category buttons to filter the sample items and select a title or “Take a closer look” to open its details.

What happens when I save an item?

The heart button marks an item as saved while the current page stays open. Saved items reset when you refresh or leave the page; nothing is stored in an account or database.

What comes next

Can I list an item or contact a seller?

Not yet. Listing items and chatting with sellers are possible future features. The How it works page explains the intended journey.

Does the contact form send a real message?

Yes. The form sends your name, email, topic, and message to Finds, where they are saved for review. The success screen appears after saving. Automatic email replies are not enabled.

What is this website built with?

Five HTML pages, shared CSS and JavaScript, and a Node.js backend using Express. The backend serves the website, provides sample listings, and saves contact messages.
