The basics

What is Finds?

Finds is a student-made prototype for discovering second-hand and pre-loved clothing. It explores how a simple and sustainable online fashion marketplace could work.

Is Finds a real marketplace?

Not yet. Finds is currently a frontend prototype. The listings, prices, and interactions are for demonstration purposes only, so no real purchases or transactions take place.

Do I need an account?

No. Every page and demo interaction is available without signing up or logging in.

Browsing & saving

Are these items available to buy?

No. All items, prices, conditions, and illustrations are samples for the prototype. No real inventory is offered for sale.

How do I explore the finds?

On the Home page, scroll to the featured collection. Use the category buttons to filter the sample items and select a title or “Take a closer look” to open its details.

What happens when I save an item?

The heart button marks an item as saved while the current page stays open. Saved items reset when you refresh or leave the page; nothing is stored in an account or database.

What comes next

Can I list an item or contact a seller?

Not yet. Listing items and chatting with sellers are possible future features. The How it works page explains the intended journey.

Does the contact form send a real message?

No. It checks the required fields and email format, then shows a demo success screen. Nothing is sent, stored, or shared.

What is this website built with?

Five HTML pages, one shared CSS file, and one shared JavaScript file. It uses local SVG illustrations and requires no framework, package installation, or backend.
